Privacy policy

Datenschutzerklärung · Last updated: 29 September 2026

In short

  • Files you open in Chartwright are processed only in your browser. They are never uploaded to us.
  • We use no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers.
  • We only store personal data if you join the waitlist: your email address, chosen plan and tools, with your consent.
  • Our website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical connection data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site.

3. Hosting and connection data

This website is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (“Cloudflare”). When you visit the site, your browser necessarily transmits technical data to Cloudflare’s servers, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested, the referring page and your browser and operating system.

Cloudflare processes this data to deliver the website, to keep it available and to protect it against attacks and misuse, for example through automated security filtering. Our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) lies in the secure and reliable operation of the website. We do not keep our own access logs and do not combine this data with other sources.

Cloudflare acts as our processor under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). For transfers to the USA, see section 9.

Program libraries

The app loads program libraries (for reading spreadsheets, drawing charts and creating PDFs) from cdnjs, a service also operated by Cloudflare. Your browser downloads these files directly, which transmits your IP address to Cloudflare, on the same legal basis as above. The font used on this website is hosted on our own website; no data is sent to Google or other font providers.

4. Using the app

When you open a file (for example Excel, CSV or JSON) in Chartwright, it is read and processed entirely in your web browser on your device. The file and its contents are not uploaded to our servers or anyone else’s, and we cannot see them.

Downloads you create, such as PDF reports or data files, are generated in your browser and saved directly to your device.

If you use “Open in my email app”, your own email program opens with a prepared message. Nothing is sent through our servers; the email is sent by you, through your email provider.

Please check your organisation’s rules before opening company data in any external tool, including Chartwright.

5. Storage on your device

We use no cookies. When you click Save in the app, the dashboard (its data, charts and settings) is stored in your browser’s local storage (IndexedDB) on your device. This data is not transmitted to us.

This storage only happens when you explicitly ask for it and is strictly necessary to provide the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). You can delete saved dashboards at any time with the Delete button on the app’s start page, or by clearing your browser’s website data.

Dashboard files you download (.chartwright.json) contain the data of your dashboard. You decide whether and with whom to share them.

6. Waitlist

You can join our waitlist to hear about the launch of paid features. We process:

  • your email address (required),
  • the plan you are interested in, and the tools you select (optional),
  • the date and time you gave your consent.

Purpose: to inform you by email about the waitlist and the launch of paid features, and to prioritise which connections we build first. We will not send you unrelated newsletters.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future, for example by emailing privacy@chartwright.de or by using the unsubscribe link in our emails. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

Storage: the data is stored in a database operated by Cloudflare (Cloudflare D1) as our processor. We delete it when you withdraw your consent, when the waitlist is no longer needed, or at the latest 24 months after your sign-up if you have not become a customer.

Providing this data is voluntary. Without an email address we cannot add you to the waitlist.

7. Contacting us

If you email us, we process your email address, name and the content of your message to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or pre-contractual steps, and otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete this data once your request is resolved, unless statutory retention periods apply.

8. Service providers

We only share personal data with service providers who process it on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA): website hosting, security, program libraries (cdnjs) and the waitlist database.

We will update this list before using additional providers, for example for sending emails or for the paid version of Chartwright.

9. Transfers outside the EU

Cloudflare is based in the USA and may process data in countries outside the European Union. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). In addition, our data processing agreement with Cloudflare includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15),
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17),
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18),
  • receive your data in a portable format (data portability, Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21),
  • withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@chartwright.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is:
