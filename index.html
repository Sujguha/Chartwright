Drop a spreadsheet. Get a dashboard.

Upload a spreadsheet, CSV or JSON file and Chartwright reads your columns, picks the right charts, and builds a dashboard you can filter and extend.

Drag your file here

Accepts Excel (.xlsx, .xlsm, .xls, .xlsb), OpenDocument (.ods), CSV, TSV, TXT and JSON. Your file is read in your browser and never sent to a server.

  1. Upload
    Use a sheet with one header row and one record per row.
  2. Review
    Key totals and charts appear automatically, based on your column types.
  3. Refine
    Filter by any category, add your own charts, and print or save as PDF.
