Questions

Is the free version really free?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser, needs no account and stays free. Paid plans add live connections and scheduled reports on top.

When will Pro be available?

It’s being built now. Waitlist members hear first and can try it before the public launch.

Does my data leave my browser?

Not in the free version: files are read on your device and never uploaded. Live connections will need secure, read-only access to your tools, hosted in the EU, and will be explained in full before launch.

Can I use it with company data?

Check your company’s policy for external tools first. Because the free version doesn’t upload anything, approval is usually simpler than for cloud tools.
